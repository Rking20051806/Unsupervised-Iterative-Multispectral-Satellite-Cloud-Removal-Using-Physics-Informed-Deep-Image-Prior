🎯 Dice & IoU (Jaccard)
Cloud Segmentation Overlap
 0 to 1.0 (Unitless)
Target: 0.85 – 0.98
 Higher is Better (↑)
1.0 = Perfect Cloud Mask Match
 $\text{Dice}=\frac{2|A \cap B|}{|A|+|B|}, \text{IoU}=\frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$ Quantifies accuracy of cloud boundary detection. High Dice ensures cloud is masked completely without falsely eroding bright building roofs or sandy terrain.

💡 Deep Dive: Purpose & Science Behind Remote Sensing Metrics

🌈 Why Spectral Angle Mapper (SAM) is essential:

In remote sensing (Sentinel-2), two pixels can have different brightness because of shadows or terrain slope, but they represent the same material if their spectral direction is identical. Standard MSE penalizes illumination differences, whereas SAM measures purely the spectral vector angle ($\theta$). A lower SAM guarantees that crop signatures and water bodies maintain true spectral signatures.

⚛️ Why Physical Realism Score (PRS) is groundbreaking:

Real-world satellite passes rarely have cloud-free ground truth taken at the exact same hour. Traditional metrics (PSNR, SSIM) fail when no ground truth is available. PRS enables No-Reference evaluation by testing whether the dehazed image mathematically satisfies radiative transfer equations and physical atmospheric attenuation profiles.

🎯 Why Cloud Dice / IoU is critical for Inpainting:

In RICE-2 (thick cloud), unmasked cloud edges leave bright halo artifacts, while over-segmentation destroys valid ground data. High Dice score confirms that the Deep Image Prior inpainting is applied strictly and exclusively where optical transmittance $t(x) \approx 0$.
