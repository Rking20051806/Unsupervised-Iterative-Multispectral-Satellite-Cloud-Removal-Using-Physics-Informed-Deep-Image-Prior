🛰️ Atmospheric Scattering Model (ASM)

The forward imaging process under cloud and aerosol cover is mathematically modeled by the Atmospheric Scattering Model (ASM):

Ib(x) = Jb(x) · tb(x) + Ab · (1 - tb(x))

Where:

  • Ib(x): Observed reflectance for spectral band b at pixel coordinate x.
  • Jb(x): Latent cloud-free surface reflectance (to be restored).
  • tb(x): Transmission map coefficient representing the fraction of light reaching the sensor without being scattered.
  • Ab: Global atmospheric light vector corresponding to the scattering particle reflectance.

⚛️ Radiative Transfer Equation (RTE) & Wavelength Coupling

To prevent spatial ambiguity between the transmission map t(x) and surface reflectance J(x), we couple the transmission maps of all bands via the physical properties of Mie and Rayleigh scattering (Beer-Lambert Law):

tb(x) = exp(-βext(λb) · d(x))

The extinction coefficient βext(λb) is modeled as a function of the central band wavelength λb using Ångström's Law:

βext(λb) = βRayleigh(λb) + cM(x) · λb-α + βabsorption(λb)

By fitting the optical depths τb(x) = -ln(tb(x)) to this equation across bands, the optimizer solves a pixel-wise linear least squares system for the physical scene depth d(x) and aerosol concentration cM(x), guaranteeing that the estimated transmission maps are physically and spectrally consistent.

🧠 Deep Image Prior (DIP) + PINN Optimization Objective

The restoration is framed as a self-supervised task where a deep neural network parameterized by θJ, θt serves as a structural prior. The model is optimized by minimizing a multi-term Physics-Informed loss function:

Ltotal = λrecon·Lrecon + λasm·Lasm + λrte·Lrte + λndvi·Lndvi + λtv·Ltv + λsam·Lsam + λt_prior·Lt_prior + λinit·Linit

Where:

  • Lrecon (Masked Reconstruction): Optimizes top-of-atmosphere matching strictly on clear-sky pixels (where mask M = 0).
  • Lasm (Unmasked ASM): Enforces the ASM forward model over the entire image. Set to 0.0 under clouds to prevent cloud leakage.
  • Lrte (Radiative Transfer Loss): Penalty for deviation from physical wavelength-dependent extinction curve.
  • Lndvi (NDVI Consistency): Penalizes deviation in the NDVI index between original and restored images to preserve vegetation characteristics.
  • Ltv (Total Variation): Penalizes high-frequency noise to enforce spatial smoothness in the transmission map.
  • Lsam (Spectral Angle Mapper): Enforces spectral angle consistency between cloudy input and restored output on clear pixels.
  • Lt_prior (Transmission Prior): Enforces high transmission (t ≈ 1) in clear pixels and penalizes high transmission in clouds.
  • Linit (Inpainting Anchor): Anchors the output surface reflectance to the initial structural inpainting on clear pixels.
