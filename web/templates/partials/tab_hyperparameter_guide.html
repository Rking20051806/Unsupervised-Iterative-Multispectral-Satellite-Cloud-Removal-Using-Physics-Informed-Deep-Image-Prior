🌤️ RICE 1: Thin Cloud & Haze

500 Samples
  • Optical Regime: Translucent thin cloud / uniform haze covering full frame. Ground terrain is partially visible ($t \in [0.35, 0.95]$).
  • Primary Loss Driver: lambda_asm = 0.50 – 0.65. Full-image Atmospheric Scattering Model ($I = J \cdot t + A(1-t)$) directly inverts radiance.
  • Transmission Constraint: Soft prior (lambda_t_prior = 0.10) targeting $t \approx 0.45$ under haze and $t \approx 0.98$ in clear zones.
  • Optimization Style: Higher learning rate ($0.005$) and low noise ($\sigma = 0.012$) for smooth global dehazing.

☁️ RICE 2: Thick Cumulus & Clouds

736 Samples
  • Optical Regime: Opaque, dense white cumulus clouds where ground radiance is completely occluded ($t \to 0$). Direct ASM inversion diverges.
  • Primary Loss Driver: Masked DIP inpainting on cloud mask $M$ with Clear Reconstruction Loss on $(1-M)$ and boundary gradient continuity.
  • ASM Role: lambda_asm = 0.04 – 0.08. Acts solely as a soft regularizer on cloud edges and transition haze.
  • Transmission Constraint: Hard prior (lambda_t_prior = 0.60 – 1.20) forcing $t \to 0$ inside thick cloud cores.
